# Chumkosoft School

Use a blue and white palette across the public site, workspace and portal. Navy frames navigation, pale blue groups related content, and strong blue signals primary actions. Keep semantic colors for attendance, warnings and errors. The white rabbit in a khao lam bamboo tube and the school grounds are the primary brand imagery. On public pages they welcome visitors. In workspaces use them where they establish context or support an empty state.

## Layout

- The public site leads with sign-in and the family and teacher guides. Give each section one job.
- Staff pages use shared navigation, a page title, a primary action and the working area. Put school attendance, devices and recent scans within reach of the people responsible for them.
- In the family portal, put the student selector and arrival status ahead of the timetable on narrow screens.
- Set Thai copy in IBM Plex Sans Thai. Allow wrapping for names, table labels and instructions. Keep important actions reachable by keyboard and touch.

## Design review

Use Tastemaker and Web Design Engineer to develop a page in the existing design system. Apply Perception Laws to group related controls and give each view a clear focal point. Use Emil Kowalski's interaction guidance and Jakub Krehel's layout guidance for predictable focus, feedback and responsive behavior. Finish with Screen Critique against desktop and mobile renders with realistic content.
